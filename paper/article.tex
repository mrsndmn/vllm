\begin{abstract}
This working draft introduces the evaluation workload for experiments on
activation and weight zeroing. We report input and output text lengths from
completed unmodified baseline runs and their repeats. These descriptive
statistics characterize the workload; they do not establish speedups or the
accuracy effects of zeroing.
\end{abstract}
